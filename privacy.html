Sunday Sort privacy policy

Sunday Sort: Driver Expenses · Published by M&A Global VN · Effective 23 November 2026

In short: Sunday Sort has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads and no analytics of our own. What you record is kept on your phone and is never sent to us. A few features use outside services (maps, address search, area names on trips, receipt reading, and backups you or your phone's system make) — listed below.

What the app stores

The entries you log (amounts, dates, notes, categories, Work/Personal), receipt photos you take, trips and mileage, and your settings. All of it is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Receipt reading runs on the phone; photos are not uploaded.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no sign-in and contains no advertising or tracking code, and we run no analytics. We do not sell, share or receive any personal data. The app does not sell anything and takes no payments. The one library that reports anonymous diagnostics to its maker is Google ML Kit (see below).

Outside services the app uses

  • Map tiles (OpenFreeMap) – when you look at a trip map, the map pictures for that area are downloaded. The recorded route itself is not sent.
  • Address search and A → B distance (openrouteservice) – only when you type or pick a place for a trip: the text you type, the places you pick, and your current position if you tap "Current location".
  • Area names on trips (your phone's map service) – to show a line such as "Leeds → Wakefield area" on a trip waiting in Sort, the app asks your phone's own address service (Google on Android, Apple on iPhone) for the town at the trip's start and end. Only those two points are sent, rounded to about 1 km – never the route itself, and not when the start or end is near a place you already saved in the app. The town names are kept on your phone.
  • Receipt reading (Google ML Kit) – the amount, shop and date are read from a receipt photo on your phone. The photo and the receipt text are not sent anywhere. As in every app that uses Google ML Kit, the library sends Google anonymous diagnostics and performance data (for example device model, app and library version, how long reading took), not linked to you.
  • System backup – your phone's own backup (Android backup to your Google account, iCloud Backup on iPhone) may include the app's data, as for most apps. It is controlled in your phone's settings, not by us.

Permissions and why

  • Location – to measure the miles you drive. Your routes stay on your phone and are never sent to us.
    • Trips you start yourself: location is used only while the trip is recording. On Android, "Allow all the time" lets the trip keep recording when the screen is off or the app is closed.
    • Automatic trips (only if you turn them on): the app uses your location in the background, even when the app is closed or not in use, to notice when you start driving and to record the trip. On Android, when the motion sensor says the phone has started moving, the app checks its position for a few minutes (up to about 25 if it keeps moving), first from the network, then from GPS. On iPhone, the system wakes the app when your location changes a lot and when you leave the spot where your last trip ended (about 150 m). The motion sensor and, if you choose, your van's Bluetooth help tell driving from walking. Positions from a check that turns out not to be a drive are deleted when the check ends; a drive's positions become that trip's route. Turning Automatic trips off stops all of this.
  • Camera / photos – only when you add a receipt photo to an entry.
  • Notifications – local reminders you turn on (weekly sort, backup, trip running). They are created on your phone; there are no push notifications.
  • Face ID / fingerprint – only if you turn on App lock. The check is done by your phone's system; the app never sees your biometric data.

Backups and exports

When you back up or export (CSV, PDF, ZIP of photos), the app creates a file and opens your phone's share sheet. You choose where it goes – for example Google Drive, iCloud Drive, email or your accountant. That service's own privacy policy then applies to the copy you send. If you turn on nightly backup, the app saves a backup file every night to the folder you chose (for example in Google Drive) and nowhere else.

Share trip diagnostics (for support) creates a file only when you ask for it. It holds what the trip detector saw in the last 7 days (motion and Bluetooth events – only your van is named, other devices are not), the times and miles of recent trips (no routes or positions), app settings and the last receipt readings. It opens your phone's share sheet and goes only where you send it.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes everything from the app. Uninstalling the app also removes it. Backup files you saved elsewhere are not affected – delete them where you saved them. Your data stays on your phone until you delete it; deleted entries and trips are kept in Recently deleted for 30 days and then removed for good.

Children

Sunday Sort is a record-keeping tool for self-employed drivers and is not directed at children.
