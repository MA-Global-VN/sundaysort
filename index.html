Sunday Sort: Driver Expenses

Receipt & mileage log for self-employed van drivers and couriers — ready for Self Assessment and MTD (UK) and Schedule C (US).

What the app does

  • Log in seconds – income or expense, the amount, a receipt photo if you like. Receipt reading on the phone fills in the amount, shop and date.
  • Sort the week on Sunday – one card at a time: pick the category, split a shared receipt, mark it Work or Personal.
  • Mileage without gaps – automatic trips when your phone notices you are driving, or Start / Stop by hand, by odometer, by distance or from A to B.
  • Ready for your tax return – tax-year totals by form line, a PDF report and files for you or your accountant (CSV, MTD records, FreeAgent file, receipt photos).

It works offline, and we never see your data: entries, receipts and trips are kept in the app on your phone.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data kept?

In the app's private storage on your phone. Backups and exports go only where you choose – for example a Google Drive or iCloud Drive folder, or your accountant's email. See the privacy policy.

An automatic trip didn't record. What should I check?

In Settings → Automatic trips, make sure it is on. Set Location to "Allow all the time" and allow Physical activity (motion on iPhone). On Android, set battery use for Sunday Sort to "Unrestricted" – the Automatic trips screen has battery tips for your phone. The app tells you when a trip stops recording and when a trip looks missing, so you can add it.

How do I move to a new phone?

On the old phone use Backup & restore → Back up now and save the file somewhere you can reach (for example Google Drive). Install Sunday Sort on the new phone, then Backup & restore → Restore from backup and pick that file.

Does Sunday Sort file my tax return?

No. It does not give tax advice and is not MTD software. It prepares totals and files that you or your accountant use or import into MTD software.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete all data removes everything from the app; uninstalling does too. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you saved them – delete them there.
